Moss Garden, an idle game for iOS, has small guests who visit the garden, and the codex has a guests chip next to the plants where we write down who has stopped by. Now and then a special guest arrives with a golden or white tint, and as guests add up, decorations that dress the garden unlock. All of this only touches how the garden looks: dew, settlement and production don't change.

## Guests stay in the codex

Guests visit even while the garden is empty. When you open the garden again, a single line in the welcome-back window shows who stopped by. On the guests chip in the codex, each guest card shows how many times they have visited and a dot if you have met a rare one. A short notice appears when a new or rare guest arrives.

## Now and then a rare guest shows up

Very rarely, a guest arrives with a golden or white tint. The weather has nothing to do with it. We don't draw new art for these; we only change the guest's color, so even the same guest can look different the moment you meet them.

## Collect enough and the garden gets a little brighter

When enough guests have visited, decorations such as halos of light, jars of fireflies and moonstones unlock, and unlocked decorations appear in the garden by themselves. They are cosmetic, and they don't earn you extra dew. So collecting at a relaxed pace costs you nothing.

## FAQ

### Do guests earn me more dew?
No. Guests and decorations only change how the garden looks; dew, settlement and production stay the same.

### Do guests visit while the app is closed?
Yes. The guests who stopped by while the garden was empty show up as one line in the welcome-back window when you open it again.
