Moss Garden has a codex. It's where the plants you've grown and the guests who have stopped by are collected, one by one. When you change seasons, the garden starts over, but the codex keeps its record.

## The codex is a record of what you've gathered

Open the codex and the plants and guests you've met are laid out for you. It starts with only a few entries, and fills up as the days of tending your garden add up.

## Changing seasons doesn't clear the codex

When you change the season, the garden starts over, but the codex and your decorations stay. Even in the new garden, you can look back at what you collected before.

## A record that fills slowly

We don't push you to fill the codex quickly. It fills a little at a time, as you look in a few times a day.

## FAQ

### How does the codex fill up?
Plant and grow plants in your garden, and the codex fills in. Guests who stop by get written in too.

### Does changing the season erase the codex?
No. The garden starts over, but the codex and your decorations stay.

### What kind of game is Moss Garden?
Moss Garden is an idle game for iOS. You plant and grow plants to gather dew.
