Moss Garden is a garden you look in on a few times a day. So it keeps growing even while the app is closed. When you come back, the welcome-back window shows the dew gathered in the meantime.

## Time away is garden time too

A garden that only grows when you tap to water it wears you out, because you have to keep coming back. In Moss Garden, plants keep making dew while you are away.

## The welcome-back window when you return

When you open the app again, a welcome-back window shows the dew gathered while you were away. Settlement has a cap, so even a long absence doesn't pile up without limit.

## Turning the clock back doesn't settle

We also stopped the trick of turning the device clock back to collect rewards. When a clock rollback is detected, that stretch is not settled.

## FAQ

### If I leave the app closed for days, does the dew pile up forever?
No. Settlement has a cap, so even after a long time away, dew only builds up to that limit.

### What kind of game is Moss Garden?
Moss Garden is an idle game for iOS. You plant and grow plants to gather dew.
