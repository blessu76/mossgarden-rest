In Moss Garden, an idle game for iOS, the plants you can grow change along with the season. The garden still has eight plant slots: four stay the same all year round, and the other four are filled by plants that bloom in the current season. Each slot keeps the same price and production per second, so the balance of the game doesn't change.

## A cherry tree in summer looks wrong

The plant list used to stay the same no matter the season. That meant a summer garden could hold both a cherry tree, which blooms in spring, and a ginkgo, which turns gold in autumn. For a game built around seasons, a garden that looked out of season bothered us.

## The slots stay, the plants in them change

We kept the order, the price and the production of the eight slots exactly as they were, and only changed which plant sits in a slot to match the season. Moss, fern, mushroom and bamboo are the base of Moss Garden, so they stay the same in every season. The other four slots (wildflowers, waterside plants, flowering trees and big trees) hold that season's plants in spring, summer, autumn and winter.

## Plants from the last season move to the matching slot

If you still have a plant that is out of season, it is replanted as the in-season plant of the same slot, with the same number of plants. The slot's price and production are the same, so your income per second doesn't drop and the price of the next plant stays put. Losing nothing was the condition for doing it this way.

## You can browse the codex by season

At the top of the codex there are spring, summer, autumn and winter chips, so you can look at the plants of each season. A plant that only blooms in another season is shown faded, with a note that you can plant it when that season comes. Plants that stand all year round carry an all-seasons mark. The season tab also tells you, before you change the season, that new plants will bloom in the next one.

## FAQ

### Do plants disappear when the season changes?
No. A plant that is out of season is replanted as the in-season plant of the same slot, with the same number of plants, so your income per second stays the same.

### Does the price of plants change with the season?
No. The price and production of each slot stay the same whatever the season.
