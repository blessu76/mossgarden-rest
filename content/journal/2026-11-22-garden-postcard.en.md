When you want to keep your garden as a single picture, use a postcard. Tap the camera button on the garden screen to open a preview, then save it to Photos or share it to another app.

## Only the garden picture is included

The postcard holds only the garden picture. The top menu, season chips, and hint text do not appear on it. The garden picture is cropped to a square from the center and fitted into the photo area of the postcard.

## What the postcard shows

Under the picture you will see the app name, the season, how many years the garden has been running, and the date you saved it. A small season stamp is printed on the right. The text follows your language.

If you have set a nickname, you can choose whether to include it. Turn off the nickname switch and the postcard is drawn again without the nickname line.

## What is not included

Dew, tap count, time of day, and location are not on the postcard. The file name is made only from the date and time.

## Permission is asked only when saving

The app asks for permission only when you save to Photos. It does not ask to read your photos, only to add to them. If you decline, a short notice appears, and you can keep playing the garden as before.

## Common questions

### Where is the postcard saved?
It is saved to Photos. Sharing opens the system share sheet.

### Does the postcard always include a nickname?
No. You can choose, and turning it off removes it from the postcard.

### What kind of game is Moss Garden?
Moss Garden is an idle game for iOS. You plant and grow plants in a garden, and collect dew as you go.
