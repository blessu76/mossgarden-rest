Moss Garden is a garden you glance at briefly on your phone, but we've also tuned it to look comfortable on larger screens like an iPad. A bigger screen doesn't mean text and cards stretch out endlessly.

## On a big screen, the garden gets bigger

On an iPad, the garden sits generously at the top of the screen, and the plant cards below it are gathered in the center. The cards don't stretch all the way to the screen edges, so they stay easy to read.

## Turning it sideways works too

Turn your iPad to landscape and the garden sits on the left, with the plant cards and menus in a panel on the right. It's nice to leave on a stand and glance at now and then.

## The codex shows more entries

When the screen is wide, the codex fits more entries in each row. You can scan the plants and guests you've met at a glance.

## On a phone, nothing changes

On an iPhone, you still see the same portrait screen as before. The layout for large screens changes only on iPad.

## FAQ

### Can I use it in landscape on an iPad?
Yes. On an iPad, even when turned sideways, the garden sits on the left and the menu on the right, so you can use it as usual.

### Does the iPhone screen change too?
No. The iPhone stays in portrait, as before.

### What kind of game is Moss Garden?
Moss Garden is an idle game for iOS. You plant and grow plants to gather dew.
